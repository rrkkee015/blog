## 1. Washington Wildfires Force Residents to Evacuate 

Large fires have destroyed hundreds of buildings in Washington state, especially near Spokane. The fires spread quickly because of dry weather and strong winds. Many people had to leave their homes to stay safe. Local firefighters and emergency workers are working hard to control the fires. The damage is severe, and it may take a long time for communities to recover. Officials warn that such wildfires could become more common with climate changes. The situation is still dangerous, and residents should stay alert if evacuation orders continue.

## 2. What You Should Know About Current U.S. Immigration Policies 

U.S. immigration rules have changed several times recently. The government has new policies that affect who can enter, stay, or get a visa. Some rules are stricter for people receiving public benefits, which means immigrants must prove they can support themselves. Others focus on speeding up legal processes and protecting workers. These changes affect many immigrants and their families. It is important to understand these new rules if you plan to move to or stay in the United States. Lawyers and community groups offer advice to help immigrants follow the law.

> ### 영어 표현 학습

> - **stricter rules** — 더 엄격한 규칙

## 3. Federal Workers Are Worried About Job Security 

Many federal government workers feel unhappy with their jobs. They say their **job security** is getting worse. This means they are afraid they might lose their jobs or that their work conditions could change. Some workers say they have less trust in the government as an employer. Low morale and worries about the future make it harder for the government to keep good employees. Experts say improving job conditions and communication might help. The government is trying to address these problems, but workers remain concerned.

> ### 영어 표현 학습

## 4. Silicon Valley Debates Closing Borders to Chinese AI 

There is a big discussion in Silicon Valley about whether the U.S. should stop Chinese companies from working with American AI technology. Some people want to **close the borders** to protect U.S. tech secrets and jobs. Others worry this will hurt business and cause **mutual suspicion** between the U.S. and China. The debate shows how important AI technology is for both countries. It also reflects fears about global competition. The government may make new rules soon, but the tech industry is divided on the best path forward.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **close the borders** — 국경을 닫다, 입국 제한하다  
>   _Some politicians want to **close the borders** to certain technologies._  
> - **protect tech secrets** — 기술 비밀을 보호하다  
>   _Companies try to **protect tech secrets** from competitors._  
> - **mutual suspicion** — 상호 불신  
>   _Trade tensions create **mutual suspicion** between countries._  
> - **reflect fears about** — ~에 대한 두려움을 반영하다  
>   _The policy changes **reflect fears about** economic security._  
> - **divided on the best path forward** — 앞으로 나아갈 최선의 길에 대해 의견이 나뉘다  
>   _The group is **divided on the best path forward** for the project._
