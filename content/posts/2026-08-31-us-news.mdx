## 1. US Launches Drone Strike on Iranian Military Sites 

The US military has **carried out** a drone attack on Iranian missile launchers located on Larak Island, near the Persian Gulf. This was the first known US strike in the region for several weeks. The attack was meant to **send a message** to Iran about its actions in the area. Officials said the strike targeted weapons that could threaten US forces or allies. This event may increase tensions between the US and Iran, which have been high for years. Experts say the situation is still **unfolding**, and more actions could follow depending on Iran’s response. Many are watching closely to see how this will affect peace and security in the Middle East.

## 2. Judge Rules Trump’s 75-Country Visa Ban Was Illegal 

A federal judge recently decided that the Trump administration’s visa ban, which blocked travelers from 75 countries, was **against the law**. The ban started in 2017 and stopped many people from entering the US. The judge said the ban was unfair and did not follow legal rules. This decision could open the door for people who were denied visas to apply again. It also raises questions about how immigration policies should be made. Many people hope this ruling will lead to more fair and clear immigration laws in the future.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **against the law** — 불법인, 법에 어긋나는  
>   _Stealing is **against the law** and can lead to punishment._  
> - **open the door for** — 가능성을 열어 주다  
>   _This new policy will **open the door for** more students to study abroad._  
> - **raise questions** — 의문을 제기하다  
>   _The report **raises questions** about the safety of the product._

## 3. US Job Numbers Revised Down, Showing Fewer New Positions Created 

Recent reports show that the US created about 79,000 **fewer jobs** than first thought in recent months. This means the job market is not as strong as people believed. However, experts say the changes are small and the overall job growth is still positive. The revised numbers make people wonder if the economy will slow down soon. Many workers watch these reports because jobs affect their lives and future plans. The government will keep updating the data to give a clearer picture of the economy.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **fewer jobs** — 더 적은 일자리  
>   _There are **fewer jobs** available in the city this year._  
> - **job market** — 취업 시장  
>   _The **job market** is very competitive for new graduates._  
> - **slow down** — 느려지다, 둔화되다  
>   _The economy may **slow down** if companies stop investing._

## 4. Silicon Valley No Longer the Top US Tech Hub 

For many years, Silicon Valley was the most famous place for technology companies in the US. But now, experts say it is no longer number one. Other cities like Austin and Miami are growing fast and attracting more tech startups and workers. This change shows that the **tech scene** in America is **expanding** beyond just one area. Companies are looking for new places with cheaper costs and good talent. This shift may change how the tech industry grows in the future and where new innovations happen.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **tech scene** — 기술 분야, IT 업계  
>   _The **tech scene** in New York is becoming stronger every year._  
> - **expanding** — 확대되는, 성장하는  
>   _The company is **expanding** its business to other countries._  
> - **shift** — 변화, 이동  
>   _There is a **shift** in consumer preferences toward online shopping._
