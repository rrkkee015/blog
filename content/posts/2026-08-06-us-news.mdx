## 1. Abdul El-Sayed Wins Michigan Senate Primary in a Key Democratic Race  


Abdul El-Sayed has won the Democratic primary for the U.S. Senate in Michigan. This race is very important because it could change the balance of power in the Senate. El-Sayed is known for his focus on healthcare and social justice issues. His victory shows strong support from voters who want new ideas and change. The seat will be important in the upcoming general election, where he will face a Republican opponent. Many people see this race as a test of the Democratic Party’s future direction. El-Sayed's win could influence how Democrats approach politics nationwide.

## 2. African States Urged to Reject Complicity in U.S. Immigration Policy  


Several African countries are being asked to stop helping the U.S. with its policy called “third country removals.” This policy means sending immigrants to a third country instead of their home country or the U.S. Human rights groups say this is unfair and hurts many people. They want African states to refuse to be part of this process. The issue is important because it affects many immigrants who face danger or poor treatment. African leaders are now under pressure to decide how they will respond. This fight highlights the challenges of immigration policies around the world.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **urge to** — ~하도록 촉구하다  
>   _The group **urged** governments **to** change the policy._  
> - **third country removals** — 제3국 추방 정책

> - **be part of** — ~의 일부가 되다

> - **under pressure** — 압박을 받다

## 3. Private-Sector Hiring Falls Short of Expectations in July  


In July, the number of jobs added by private companies was less than experts expected. This report shows that companies hired fewer people than predicted. Still, the job market is doing okay overall. Some industries are growing while others slow down. This news is important because it tells us how the economy is doing and how many people can find work. Experts will watch the next reports closely to see if this is a short-term problem or a bigger issue. For workers, it means the job market might stay **resilient** but also a bit uncertain.

## 4. China’s Tech Advances Challenge Silicon Valley and U.S. Government  


China is making big progress in technology, which is causing concern from Silicon Valley to the White House. These advances include areas like artificial intelligence and 5G technology. Many in the U.S. worry that China’s growth could hurt American businesses or national security. This has led to debates about how to respond, including new rules and restrictions. The situation shows how technology is important for global power. It also raises questions about trust and cooperation between countries in the tech world.
