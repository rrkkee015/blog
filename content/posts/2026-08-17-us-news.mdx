## 1. US to Scale Back Military Drills with South Korea After Iran War Standoff  


The US government announced it will **scale back** military drills with South Korea. This decision comes after South Korea decided not to join the US in any action related to the Iran war. Military drills are important to keep troops ready and to show strength. But now, the US feels South Korea did not fully **support** their plans. This change might affect the relationship between the two countries. Experts wonder if this will make North Korea feel less threatened. The US says it still wants to keep peace but needs to **adjust** its strategies. We will watch closely to see what happens next.

## 2. Job Market Sees Surprising Changes in Who Struggles to Find Work  


A new analysis by the Federal Reserve shows changes in the US job market. Some groups of people are now **struggling** more to find jobs than before. For example, younger workers and those with less experience have more trouble. However, the number of job openings dropped in June, but hiring actually increased. This means companies are hiring more but may be more careful about who they choose. The summer jobs report shows a **double whammy** for workers: fewer openings but more competition. This is important because it affects many families and the economy. Experts say workers will need to **adapt** to these new challenges.

## 3. China’s Tech Advances Shake Up Silicon Valley and Washington

But others are divided because they want to keep working with China. This situation shows how technology affects politics and business globally. It could change how the US competes in the future. Many experts say this is a complex issue that needs smart decisions.
