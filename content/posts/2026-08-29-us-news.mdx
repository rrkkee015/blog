## 1. California Couple Survives Deadly Flood in Nepal 

A California couple told their story about surviving a terrible flood in Nepal. The flood was very strong and dangerous. The couple said, "**I knew I was going to die**" because the water was rising fast and they were trapped. Many people lost their homes and lives in this disaster. The flood happened after heavy rain caused a river to overflow. This story shows how natural events can be very sudden and serious. It also reminds us to be careful and prepared in dangerous weather. Relief groups are working to help the survivors and rebuild the area.

## 2. Federal Court Rules ICE Detention Policy Unlawful 

A federal appeals court in Philadelphia decided that the ICE policy of **mandatory detention** is not legal. ICE is the agency that controls immigration enforcement in the US. The policy forced some immigrants to stay in detention centers without a chance for release. The court said this breaks the law and the immigrants’ rights. This ruling may change how immigration cases are handled in the future. People who support immigrant rights are happy with this decision. The government might have to review its detention rules and follow the court’s order.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **mandatory detention** — 의무 구금  
>   _The court said **mandatory detention** without review is unfair._  
> - **not legal** — 합법적이지 않은  
>   _The judge found the policy **not legal** under current laws._  
> - **breaks the law** — 법을 위반하다  
>   _Forcing people to stay without hearing **breaks the law**._  
> - **immigrants’ rights** — 이민자의 권리  
>   _Protecting **immigrants’ rights** is important in this case._

## 3. US Employment Growth Slightly Lower Than Expected 

New data shows that US job growth is only a little bit smaller than first thought. The Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) updated their numbers and said the US added fewer jobs in the last year, but the difference is **modest**. This means the job market is still healthy but not growing very fast. Many experts watch these numbers because they show how the economy is doing. Slower job growth can affect people’s income and spending. Still, the news is not very bad and some industries are hiring more workers. The government will keep watching the situation carefully.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **modestly lower** — 약간 낮은

## 4. America’s Tech Hubs Are Growing Beyond Silicon Valley 

The US tech industry is changing. Silicon Valley is no longer the only place where big tech companies and startups grow. Other cities and regions across America are becoming new **tech hubs**. These places offer good jobs, innovation, and new ideas. Many young people and professionals are moving to these areas. This spread helps the tech industry grow faster and reach more people. It also lowers the cost of living compared to Silicon Valley. This trend may change where future tech companies start and where jobs are available.

> - **innovation** — 혁신
