## 1. U.S. Launches New Airstrikes in Iran 

The United States has started fresh airstrikes in Iran. This action comes amid rising tensions between the two countries over security and political issues. The U.S. government says these strikes target groups that threaten American interests in the region. This move is important because it may affect peace and stability in the Middle East. Many experts worry this could lead to more conflict or retaliation. The situation is still changing, and people around the world are watching carefully. The U.S. says it wants to protect its forces and allies while avoiding a wider war.

## 2. Trump’s Immigration Policies Make Citizenship Harder 

New reports show that immigration policies from the Trump era have made it more difficult for people to get and keep U.S. citizenship. These policies added rules and checks that slow down the process. Some immigrants now face more questions and investigations before they can become citizens. This matters because citizenship gives people rights and security in the United States. Many immigrant advocates say these changes make life harder for families who want to belong here. Lawmakers are discussing ways to protect immigrants and make the system fairer and easier to understand.

>   _The company **added rules and checks** to improve safety._  
> - **face more questions** — 더 많은 질문을 받다

> - **make life harder** — 삶을 더 어렵게 만들다

## 3. US Job Growth Slows as Economy Adds Only 17,000 Jobs 

The latest jobs report shows the U.S. economy added just 17,000 jobs in February 2026. This is a much smaller number than expected. Experts say the job market is slowing down, and hiring is not as strong as before. This can affect many people, especially new college graduates who want to find work. Some companies are cautious about hiring because of economic uncertainty. Despite this, certain industries like technology and healthcare still show some growth. The overall picture suggests the economy may be entering a slower phase.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **job growth slows** — 일자리 증가가 둔화되다  
>   _After the holiday season, **job growth slows** in many sectors._  
> - **added just 17,000 jobs** — 단 17,000개의 일자리가 추가되다  
>   _The factory **added just 17,000 jobs** last month._  
> - **job market** — 취업 시장  
>   _The **job market** is competitive for new graduates._  
> - **economic uncertainty** — 경제적 불확실성  
>   _Many businesses delay decisions due to **economic uncertainty**._  
> - **entering a slower phase** — 느린 단계에 들어가다  
>   _The economy is **entering a slower phase** after rapid growth._

## 4. Silicon Valley Debates Closing Borders to Chinese AI Companies 

In Silicon Valley, leaders and experts are divided over whether the U.S. should close its borders to Chinese artificial intelligence (AI) companies. Some say this is necessary to protect technology and national security. Others worry that cutting off Chinese firms will hurt innovation and business cooperation. The debate comes as AI grows fast and becomes more important worldwide. The U.S. government is considering new rules about who can work with Chinese AI companies. This issue shows how technology and politics are closely linked today.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **closing the borders** — 국경을 닫다, 입국을 제한하다  
>   _The country is **closing the borders** to stop the virus._  
> - **protect technology and national security** — 기술과 국가 안보를 보호하다  
>   _New laws aim to **protect technology and national security**._  
> - **hurt innovation** — 혁신을 해치다  
>   _Too many rules may **hurt innovation** in the tech industry._  
> - **business cooperation** — 사업 협력  
>   _Good **business cooperation** helps companies grow._  
> - **technology and politics are closely linked** — 기술과 정치가 밀접하게 연결되어 있다  
>   _In today’s world, **technology and politics are closely linked**._
