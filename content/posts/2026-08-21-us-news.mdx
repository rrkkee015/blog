## 1. US Plans Toughest Sanctions Ever on Iran, Urges China to Help  
  

The US government says it will put the **toughest** sanctions ever on Iran. These sanctions are strong economic and political actions to pressure Iran about its nuclear program. The US also asked China to **cooperate** and help enforce these rules. China’s support is important because it trades a lot with Iran. This news is part of a bigger fight over Iran’s influence in the Middle East. If China helps, the sanctions could be more effective. But if China does not **cooperate**, the US may face challenges in making the sanctions work well. This situation might affect global politics and trade soon.

## 2. Trump’s Immigration Policy Reminds Us of the 1920s  
  

The New York Times reports that President Trump’s immigration policy is similar to rules from the 1920s. Back then, the US limited certain groups of newcomers from entering the country. Today, the policy focuses on reducing legal immigration from some countries and groups. This shows a return to strict immigration controls. The policy has caused many debates about fairness and the future of immigration in the US. Some people say the rules protect American jobs, while others believe they are too harsh. This policy could change the makeup of new immigrants in the coming years. It is important to understand this history to see how immigration affects the country.

## 3. US Job Market Shows Mixed Signs: Claims Down but Concerns Remain  
  

Recent reports show that US unemployment claims went down last week. This means fewer people asked for unemployment benefits, which is a good sign. It suggests some **stability** in the job market. But other reports say job losses in July were higher than first thought. This shows the labor market might be **weakening**. A healthy job market helps the economy grow and people feel secure. Experts watch these numbers closely to understand if the economy is improving or facing problems. The mixed news means we should be careful but hopeful that jobs will get better soon.  

> ### 영어 표현 학습

Others say the rules protect national security. The divide shows how important AI is for the US and China. Many tech leaders want to **unite** and find a way to keep innovation going. But the Chinese AI models are at the center of this disagreement. What happens next could shape the future of AI development and global technology competition.
