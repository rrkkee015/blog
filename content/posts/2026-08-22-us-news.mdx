## 1. Silicon Valley Divided on Closing Borders to Chinese AI Models  


Silicon Valley, the center of technology in the US, is **split** over whether to close borders to Chinese artificial intelligence (AI) models. Some tech companies want to **limit** access to these powerful but low-cost AI tools because of security and economic concerns. Others believe that blocking these models could slow down innovation and hurt businesses. The debate shows how technology and politics are **closely linked** today. This issue matters because AI is becoming very important in many industries, and decisions now will shape the future of tech development. In the coming months, we may see new rules or policies that affect how AI technology is shared across countries.

## 2. Trump’s Immigration Policy Echoes 1920s Attitudes  


The current US immigration policy under former President Trump has **echoed** the attitudes of the 1920s. During that time, the US limited the number of newcomers from certain countries because of fear and prejudice. Today, some policies restrict legal immigration and favor certain groups over others. Experts say this approach may harm America’s diversity and economy in the long run. The story helps us understand how history can **repeat itself** and why immigration rules are so important in shaping the country’s future. People are watching to see if the government will change these policies or keep them as they are.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **echo** — (과거 사건 등을) 반영하다, 되풀이하다  
>   _The new law **echoes** earlier policies from the 20th century._  
> - **repeat itself** — 반복되다  
>   _History often **repeats itself** if we do not learn from it._  
> - **long run** — 장기적으로  
>   _Investing in education pays off in the **long run**._

## 3. Hiring Slows Down but Economy Shows Hope  


In June, the US job market saw fewer new hires than experts expected. This means companies are hiring more slowly now. However, the White House said the labor market is still on an **upward trajectory**. This means they believe jobs and the economy will continue to get better in the near future. Some people worry this slowdown could be a sign of economic problems ahead. But others think it is normal after a strong recovery. How the job market changes in the next few months will be important for the whole US economy.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **upward trajectory** — 상승하는 경향  
>   _The company’s sales are on an **upward trajectory** this year._  
> - **slow down** — 느려지다  
>   _Traffic tends to **slow down** during rush hour._  
> - **sign of** — ~의 신호  
>   _A drop in sales can be a **sign of** trouble._

## 4. Trade Deal Could Have 'Bad Outcome' for US and Canada  


A recent trade deal between the US and Canada may lead to a **bad outcome**, according to a former Canadian minister. He said the agreement does not protect important industries well and could hurt workers in both countries. Trade deals are important because they affect jobs, prices, and the economy. Many people are watching this deal closely because the US and Canada have a long history of trading with each other. The future may bring changes or new talks to fix the problems in the agreement.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **bad outcome** — 좋지 않은 결과  
>   _The project had a **bad outcome** because of poor planning._  
> - **protect** — 보호하다, 지키다  
>   _Laws are made to **protect** consumers._  
> - **affect** — 영향을 미치다
