## 1. ICE Airport Arrests Rise Sharply, Creating New Challenges 

The U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) has increased the number of arrests at airports across the country. This is part of a **deportation drive** to remove more undocumented immigrants. Airports are now a major focus for ICE agents, which has surprised many travelers and raised concerns. The increase is meant to make it harder for people without legal permission to enter or stay in the U.S. Some critics say this approach can cause fear and confusion for travelers. The government says it is **necessary** to enforce immigration laws and protect the country. The situation may lead to more debates about immigration policies and airport security soon.

## 2. U.S. Job Market Shows Slow Growth in February 2026 

The latest jobs report shows the U.S. economy added only 17,000 new jobs in February 2026. This number is much lower than experts expected. The **job market** is slowing down, especially for new college graduates who find it harder to get work. Some industries still hire, but overall growth is weak. This slow growth could affect wages and how people plan their careers. Economists say the government will watch these numbers closely to decide on future economic policies. The slow job growth may signal more changes in the economy soon.

## 4. U.S. Links Iran to Cyberattacks on Minnesota Water Systems 

The U.S. government believes Iran is likely behind recent cyberattacks on water systems in Minnesota. These attacks targeted important facilities that provide clean water to people. Cybersecurity experts warn that such attacks can harm public safety and cause serious problems. The U.S. is trying to improve its defenses against these threats. Officials say the attacks show how countries use **cyber warfare** to cause damage without physical conflict. This event raises concerns about protecting critical infrastructure from hackers in the future.
