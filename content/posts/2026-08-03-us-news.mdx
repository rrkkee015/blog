## 1. Wildfires Destroy Hundreds of Buildings in Spokane, Causing Mass Evacuations  


This summer, wildfires in Spokane have destroyed hundreds of buildings. Thousands of people had to leave their homes to stay safe. The fires spread quickly because of dry weather and strong winds. Emergency teams are working hard to control the flames, but the situation is still dangerous. Many families lost their homes and need help from the government and local organizations. Officials warn that the fire season may get worse if the dry conditions continue. This disaster shows how important it is to prepare for natural events and protect the environment. People in nearby areas are asked to stay alert and follow evacuation orders if needed.

## 2. Court Rejects Trump’s Mandatory Immigration Detention Policy  


A US court in the 7th Circuit recently rejected a rule that would force immigrants to be detained while waiting for their cases. This policy was introduced during the Trump administration to keep migrants in jail instead of allowing them to wait outside. The court said the rule was too strict and did not follow proper legal procedures. This decision is important because it changes how immigration cases are handled in the US. Many immigrants could now have more freedom while they wait. The ruling may affect other immigration policies and how future cases are managed. Immigration groups welcomed the decision, saying it respects human rights. The government might appeal this ruling or create new rules.  

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **mandatory detention** — 의무 구금  
>   _The court rejected the **mandatory detention** rule._  
> - **legal procedures** — 법적 절차  
>   _Policies must follow correct **legal procedures**._  
> - **appeal this ruling** — 이 판결에 항소하다  
>   _The government may **appeal this ruling** in a higher court._  
> - **respect human rights** — 인권을 존중하다  
>   _The new decision helps **respect human rights**._

## 3. US Job Market Shows Mixed Signals in Early 2026

## 4. China’s Tech Advances Create Challenges for Silicon Valley and Washington  


China has made big progress in technology, especially in areas like artificial intelligence and computer chips. This growth is causing concern in Silicon Valley and the US government. Some American tech companies worry that China’s advances will create more competition. The US government is also thinking about new rules that could limit technology sharing with China. These tensions could affect business, innovation, and international relations. Some experts say cooperation is still important, but others warn about the risks of sharing too much. The debate will continue as both countries try to protect their economic interests. This situation shows how technology plays a big role in global politics today.
