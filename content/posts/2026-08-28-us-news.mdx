## 1. The Rise of New Tech Hubs Outside Silicon Valley  


The U.S. tech industry is **expanding beyond** Silicon Valley. For many years, Silicon Valley was the center of technology innovation and startups. But now, other cities like Austin, Miami, and Denver are becoming important places for tech companies. This change is because workers and companies want **more affordable** places to live and work. Also, the pandemic made remote work more common, so people do not need to stay in one place. This shift could **change the job market** and how technology grows in the U.S. Cities outside California are working hard to attract tech talent and investment.

> - **attract talent** — 인재를 끌어들이다

## 2. New U.S. Immigration Policies Pause Visa Applications  


The U.S. government has **paused all immigrant visa applications** because of a new screening policy. This policy is meant to improve security at the border. But it also means many people who want to come to the U.S. must wait longer or cannot apply right now. The change affects families and workers from many countries. Some experts worry this will **slow down immigration** and cause problems for businesses that need workers. The government says the policy will help protect the country, but it is a difficult balance. People will watch carefully to see how this affects immigration next year.

>   _New laws might **slow down immigration** for some groups._  
> - **a difficult balance** — 어려운 균형  
>   _Protecting freedom and safety is **a difficult balance**._

## 3. Summer Jobs Report Brings a Double Whammy for Workers  


The latest summer jobs report shows a **double whammy** for workers. First, job openings have dropped, meaning there are fewer new jobs available. Second, many workers face higher prices for everyday things like food and gas. This combination makes it hard for people to find good jobs and save money. Some companies are hiring more, but others are cutting back because of global problems like the Iran war. Unemployment is low at 4.1%, but many workers still feel pressure. The summer job market will be important to watch as the year continues.  

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **double whammy** — 이중 타격  
>   _Losing a job and facing high bills is a **double whammy**._  
> - **job openings** — 일자리 공석  
>   _There are many **job openings** in the tech industry._  
> - **cutting back** — (규모를) 줄이다, 축소하다  
>   _The company is **cutting back** on hiring this year._  
> - **feel pressure** — 압박을 느끼다  
>   _Many students **feel pressure** before exams._

## 4. Far-Right Ideas Influence U.S. Immigration Policy  


A far-right idea called **remigration** is starting to influence U.S. immigration policy. Remigration means encouraging or forcing immigrants to leave the country and return to their original homes. Some politicians and groups support this because they want fewer immigrants in the U.S. This idea is controversial and causes a lot of debate. Critics say it is unfair and could harm families and communities. Supporters say it helps protect national identity and jobs. This topic is important because it could change immigration rules in the future.  

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **remigration** — 재이민, 이주민을 다시 본국으로 보내는 것  
>   _The group supports **remigration** for immigrants who do not have legal papers._  
> - **causes a lot of debate** — 많은 논쟁을 일으키다  
>   _The new law **causes a lot of debate** among citizens._  
> - **protect national identity** — 국가 정체성을 보호하다  
>   _Some policies aim to **protect national identity**._  
> - **change immigration rules** — 이민 규칙을 바꾸다  
>   _The government plans to **change immigration rules** next year._
