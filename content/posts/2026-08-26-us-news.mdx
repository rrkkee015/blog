## 1. Canada Hits Back with $20 Billion in Tariffs Against US Goods  
  

Trade tensions between the United States and Canada are getting worse. Canada has put new **retaliatory tariffs** on US goods worth $20 billion. This means Canada is charging extra taxes on many American products. This move is a response to US tariffs that Canada says are unfair. It affects many industries, including steel, aluminum, and agriculture. These tariffs could make goods more expensive for consumers in both countries. Businesses on both sides are worried about the impact on trade and jobs. The dispute might continue if the two countries cannot find a solution soon.

## 2. New Immigration Policies Inspired by Far-Right Ideas  
  

The idea of **remigration** is starting to influence US immigration policy. Remigration means encouraging some immigrants to return to their home countries. This idea comes mainly from far-right groups who want stricter immigration controls. Some politicians are now supporting laws based on this idea. Critics say it could harm immigrant communities and cause social problems. Supporters argue it will protect American jobs and culture. The debate is growing as the US faces challenges with immigration numbers and border control. What happens next could change many lives.

## 4. America’s Tech Growth Moves Beyond Silicon Valley  
  

Silicon Valley is famous as the US tech hub, but that is changing. Today, many tech companies and startups are growing in other cities across America. Places like Austin, Seattle, and Denver are becoming new centers for technology. This shift happens because of lower costs and more space outside Silicon Valley. It also means more job opportunities in different parts of the country. Experts say this trend will continue, making the US tech scene more spread out. This change could help smaller cities grow and bring more innovation nationwide.  

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **tech hub** — 기술 중심지  (two trailing spaces)  
>   _New York is becoming a major **tech hub** in the East._  
> - **startups** — 신생 기업들  (two trailing spaces)  
>   _Many **startups** choose cities with lower living costs._  
> - **shift happens** — 변화가 일어나다  (two trailing spaces)  
>   _A **shift happens** when companies move to new locations._
