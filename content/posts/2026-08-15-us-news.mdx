## 1. US Economy Loses 23,000 Jobs in July: Hiring Goes into Reverse  


The US economy lost 23,000 jobs in July, a surprising change after months of steady growth. This means **hiring has gone into reverse** — companies are now cutting jobs instead of adding new ones. This news worries many people because a strong job market usually means a healthy economy. Experts say this could slow down economic growth and affect people's daily lives. It is also a political setback for the current government, which often focuses on job numbers. The reasons for the loss include inflation and higher interest rates. Economists will watch closely to see if this trend continues in the coming months.

## 2. Trump’s Immigration Policy Echoes 1920s Crackdown Without Congress  


Former President Trump’s immigration policies are being compared to a strict crackdown from the 1920s. This time, Congress is not involved in making the decisions, which is unusual. The policies focus on reducing immigration and increasing deportations. Many people worry this approach causes fear and stress in immigrant communities. Experts say these policies may harm people's mental health and create social problems. The government defends the policies as necessary for national security. However, immigration groups plan to continue pushing for more humane laws in the future.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **crackdown** — 단속, 강력한 조치  
>   _The city government announced a **crackdown** on illegal parking._  
> - **without Congress** — 의회 없이  
>   _The new rule was made **without Congress** approval._  
> - **mental health toll** — 정신 건강에 미치는 영향

## 3. Silicon Valley Divided Over China’s Powerful AI Models  


In Silicon Valley, people have different opinions about China’s advances in artificial intelligence (AI). Some see China’s cheap and powerful AI models as a big threat to US tech companies. Others worry that closing borders to Chinese AI experts could slow down innovation. The debate shows how important AI technology is for future business and national security. The US government and tech leaders are trying to find the best way to protect their technology while staying open to talent. This split could affect how America competes with China in the tech world.

## 4. Trump Dismisses Families’ Concerns About USS Abraham Lincoln Conditions  


Former President Trump responded to families worried about the conditions aboard the USS Abraham Lincoln aircraft carrier. Some families said the living spaces and health conditions were poor. Trump dismissed these concerns, saying the ship was well managed and safe. The issue is important because the ship has many service members living there during the COVID-19 pandemic. Military officials are looking into the complaints to make sure everyone is safe. Families want better communication and support from the military in the future.
