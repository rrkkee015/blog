## 1. Women Drive Most of August’s U.S. Job Gains 

In August, women made up almost 98% of the new jobs created in the United States. This shows a strong **job market** where many women are finding work. The labor market is important because it affects the economy and people’s lives. When more people have jobs, the economy usually grows and people can spend money. This large share of job growth by women may change how companies hire and support workers. Understanding who is helping the economy grow helps leaders make better decisions. The focus now is on how these changes might affect inflation and future job growth.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **job market** — 노동 시장  
>   _The **job market** is competitive this year._  
> - **job gains** — 일자리 증가  
>   _The company reported strong **job gains** last quarter._  
> - **labor market** — 노동 시장 (broader than job market)  
>   _The **labor market** is affected by many factors._

## 2. Immigration Policy Faces New Challenges This Week 

This week, immigration policy in the U.S. continues to be a hot topic. Some groups want to change rules about who can stay or come back to the U.S. One idea called **remigration** is gaining attention. It means encouraging some immigrants to return to their home countries. This idea is controversial and affects many people’s lives. Congress is also being watched because some say it has not done enough to control immigration. The midterm elections may change how immigration laws are handled next. This topic is important because it affects families, workers, and the country’s future.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **immigration policy** — 이민 정책  
>   _The government is reviewing its **immigration policy**._  
> - **remigration** — 재이민 (고국으로 돌아가는 것)  
>   _Some politicians support **remigration** as a solution._  
> - **midterm elections** — 중간 선거  
>   _The **midterm elections** could change the government’s approach._

## 3. Silicon Valley and U.S. Face Urgency Over Taiwan 

The U.S. and Silicon Valley leaders are worried about Taiwan’s future. Taiwan is very important for the tech world because many computer parts come from there. Experts say the U.S. may be **running out of time** to prepare for problems in that region. If there is a conflict, it could affect global technology and the economy. Silicon Valley companies want the U.S. to act quickly to protect Taiwan and their business. This situation shows how world politics and technology are connected. People will watch closely what the U.S. government decides next.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **running out of time** — 시간이 부족하다  
>   _We are **running out of time** to finish the project._  
> - **silicon valley** — 실리콘밸리 (미국의 기술 산업 중심지)  
>   _Many tech startups are based in **Silicon Valley**._  
> - **tech world** — 기술 세계  
>   _The **tech world** changes very fast._

## 4. States Challenge Tech Lobby on AI Rules 

Many U.S. states are creating their own rules for artificial intelligence (AI). They do this even though big tech companies want different laws. This shows that the **landscape has changed dramatically** in how AI is controlled. States want to protect people and keep AI safe. This is important because AI is growing fast and affects daily life. The fight over AI rules shows that technology and law need to work together. We will see if the federal government will create a national plan or if states will lead the way.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **landscape has changed dramatically** — 상황이 크게 변했다  
>   _The **landscape has changed dramatically** in online learning._  
> - **tech lobby** — 기술 업계 로비 단체  
>   _The **tech lobby** tries to influence new laws._  
> - **artificial intelligence (AI)** — 인공지능  
>   _Many companies use **artificial intelligence** to improve services._
