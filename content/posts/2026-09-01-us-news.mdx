## 1. Army Secretary Dan Driscoll Steps Down from His Position  


Dan Driscoll, the Army Secretary, has decided to **resign** from his post. This means he is leaving his job before the usual time. Driscoll said this was a personal choice, but it comes during a time of change in the Army’s leadership. The Army Secretary plays an important role by helping to manage the Army’s resources and policies. His resignation might affect ongoing projects and plans inside the Army. The government will soon look for a new person to fill this important position. This change could bring new ideas or challenges depending on who takes the role next. People are watching to see how this will impact the Army’s future.

## 2. Judge Rules Trump’s 75-Country Visa Ban Was Illegal  


A judge has ruled that the Trump administration’s visa ban on 75 countries was **illegal**. This ban stopped many people from those countries from entering the United States. The judge said the ban did not follow the law properly. This decision is important because it affects immigration rules and many travelers who were blocked. It also shows how courts can check government actions. The ruling may lead to changes in future immigration policies. People are waiting to see if the government will accept this decision or try to appeal it.

> - **illegal** — 불법의, 법에 어긋나는

## 3. U.S. Companies Continue Hiring Offshore Workers Despite AI Growth  


Even though artificial intelligence (AI) is growing fast, many U.S. companies still hire **cheap offshore workers**. Offshore workers are employees who live and work in other countries. Companies use AI to improve efficiency, but they also want to save money by hiring workers from places with lower wages. This trend shows that AI has not replaced all human jobs yet. It also raises questions about the future of the job market in the U.S. Some experts say companies balance using AI and affordable labor to stay competitive. It will be interesting to see how this affects American workers in the coming years.

> ### 영어 표현 학습

## 4. The Top U.S. Tech Hub Is No Longer Silicon Valley  


Silicon Valley has been known as the top tech hub in the U.S. for many years. But now, the No. 1 tech area is changing. New cities and regions are growing fast in technology and startups. This means that more places outside Silicon Valley are becoming important for tech jobs and innovation. The shift could bring new opportunities for people living in different parts of the country. It also shows how the tech industry is spreading out and not staying in just one place. This change may affect where new companies start and where tech workers choose to live.
