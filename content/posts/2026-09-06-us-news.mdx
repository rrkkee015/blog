## 1. U.S. Military Hits Three Iranian Tankers After Navy Action 

The U.S. military reported that it **hit** three Iranian tankers following a recent attack on Navy ships. This event happened in a tense area where U.S. forces are working to keep peace and protect shipping lanes. The Iranian tankers were **targeted** because they were believed to be carrying oil to Syria, which is under sanctions. This military action shows how the U.S. is trying to stop activities that may break international rules. Experts say this could increase tensions between the U.S. and Iran. The situation is important because it affects global oil supplies and regional security. We will need to watch closely to see how Iran responds next.

## 2. A Week of Important Immigration News 

This week in U.S. immigration, several key topics have come up. First, the idea of **remigration** is gaining attention. This means some people want immigrants to return to their home countries. This idea comes from far-right political groups and is influencing immigration policy. Second, many stories show how harsh immigration rules have created serious problems for families and individuals. These policies affect many people’s lives and futures. Immigration is a big topic because it affects the economy, communities, and America’s global image. Understanding these issues helps us see why immigration policy is so important today.

## 3. August Job Growth Boosts Focus on Inflation 

In August, the U.S. added 162,000 new jobs, a number that surprised many experts. This strong **job market** shows that businesses are hiring again even as inflation remains a concern. Inflation means prices for goods and services are rising, which affects how much people can buy. The report shows that the economy is still growing but also makes the Federal Reserve think carefully about interest rates. Another interesting fact is that women made up 98% of these new job gains, showing their important role in the labor force. This news matters because jobs and inflation have a big effect on everyday life for many Americans.

## 4. America’s Tech Industry Is Changing Fast 

America’s technology scene is expanding beyond Silicon Valley, the famous tech hub. Many new tech companies are growing in different states. This shift is important because it spreads jobs and innovation across the country. At the same time, states are starting to create their own rules for artificial intelligence (AI), which is changing the **landscape** of the tech world. These new rules often go against the wishes of big tech companies, showing that the industry is facing new challenges. How America handles these changes will affect its future as a tech leader worldwide.

> ### 영어 표현 학습
