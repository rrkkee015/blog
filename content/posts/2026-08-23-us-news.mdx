## 1. Judge Rules Trump’s 75-Country Visa Ban Was Illegal 

A U.S. judge recently made an important decision about a visa ban from the Trump administration. This ban stopped people from 75 countries from entering the United States. The judge said the ban was **illegal** because it did not follow the proper rules. Many people affected by the ban had their travel plans canceled or delayed. This decision could change how the government makes future visa policies. It also shows how courts can check government actions. The ruling may reopen doors for travelers and immigrants from these countries.

## 2. Trump’s Immigration Policy Reminds Us of the 1920s 

Trump’s immigration rules seem to bring back ideas from the 1920s. Back then, the U.S. was very careful about who could come into the country. The policy focuses on stopping certain groups of newcomers. This can affect families and workers who want to move to the U.S. Some people say this policy is too strict and unfair. Understanding this history helps us see why immigration is still a big and sensitive topic. It shows how past ideas can come back in new forms.

## 3. US Job Market Shows Stability with Lower Unemployment Claims 

Last week, fewer people asked for unemployment benefits in the United States. This means the job market is **stable** right now. When unemployment claims go down, it usually shows that more people have jobs. This is good news for workers and businesses. However, some experts warn that there are still challenges ahead. The job market can change quickly depending on the economy. Keeping an eye on these numbers helps us understand how healthy the economy is.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **job market** — 취업 시장

> - **unemployment claims** — 실업 수당 신청  
>   _The number of **unemployment claims** dropped last week._  
> - **stable** — 안정된

## 4. Silicon Valley Divided Over Closing Borders to Chinese AI 

There is a big debate in Silicon Valley about Chinese artificial intelligence (AI). Some people want to **close the borders** to Chinese AI technology because of security and competition concerns. Others say that this could slow down innovation and hurt cooperation between countries. The debate shows how technology can create political and business challenges. AI from China is powerful and often cheaper than alternatives. What happens next could affect the future of AI development worldwide.
