## 1. Judge Rules Trump’s 75-Country Visa Ban Was Illegal  


A U.S. judge recently decided that the Trump administration’s visa ban affecting 75 countries was **illegal**. The ban stopped many people from entering the United States, citing security concerns. However, the court found that the government did not follow the proper rules before putting the ban in place. This decision is important because it affects many families and workers who were blocked from coming to the U.S. The ruling may lead to changes in how future immigration policies are made. It also raises questions about how the government can balance security with fairness. For now, people from these countries might have a better chance of getting visas again.

## 2. Shipping Traffic in Strait of Hormuz Remains Low Due to US Sanctions  


The Strait of Hormuz is a very important waterway for global oil shipping. Recently, the traffic of ships there has been **below normal** because of U.S. sanctions on Iran. These sanctions put pressure on Iran’s economy and reduce its oil exports. Because of this, fewer ships are passing through the strait, which affects global oil supply. The situation is tense because the strait is narrow and has been a spot for conflicts in the past. Many countries watch this area closely because any disruption could raise oil prices worldwide. The U.S. sanctions show how political actions can impact global trade routes.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **below normal** — 정상 이하의  
>   _Ship traffic was **below normal** due to economic problems._  
> - **sanctions** — 제재  
>   _The country faced **sanctions** that limited its trade._  
> - **put pressure on** — 압박하다  
>   _The new rules **put pressure on** the company to change._  
> - **global oil supply** — 전 세계 석유 공급  
>   _Changes in shipping affect the **global oil supply**._

## 3. Silicon Valley Is No Longer America’s Top Tech Hub  


For many years, Silicon Valley was known as the number one tech center in the U.S. But now, that is changing. New reports show that other cities are growing fast and attracting more technology companies. Places like Austin, Texas, and Seattle have become popular for tech workers because of lower costs and good quality of life. This shift is important because it changes where tech jobs and innovation happen in America. The move also affects local economies and housing markets. Experts say this trend will continue as companies look for new locations to grow.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **tech hub** — 기술 중심지  
>   _New York is becoming a big **tech hub** for startups._  
> - **attract** — 끌어들이다  
>   _The city tries to **attract** new businesses with tax benefits._  
> - **quality of life** — 삶의 질  
>   _Many people move to places with a higher **quality of life**._  
> - **shift** — 변화, 이동  
>   _There is a big **shift** from the city to the suburbs._

## 4. U.S. Employment Growth Revised: Fewer Jobs Created Than First Reported  


The U.S. government recently updated its job numbers and found that **job creation** was less than originally thought. The revision showed about 79,000 fewer jobs were made in the past year. This news is important because employment numbers show how strong the economy is. Lower job growth can mean slower economic growth and affect people looking for work. Experts say this revision helps give a clearer picture of the job market. It also may influence future government policies about the economy and jobs.
