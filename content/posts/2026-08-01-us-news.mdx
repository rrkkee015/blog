## 1. New Challenges in U.S. Immigration Policy Amid Migrant Surge  


The city of Ceuta, a Spanish territory near Morocco, has seen a large increase in migrants trying to cross its borders. This surge has caused officials to think about **restrictive immigration policies**. These policies are rules that make it harder for people to enter and stay in a country. The goal is to control the number of migrants and protect resources. However, these policies also raise questions about human rights and safety for migrants. The situation is complicated because many migrants are escaping difficult conditions in their home countries. The government needs to find a balance between security and compassion as the problem grows.

## 2. US Job Market Shows Slow Growth in February 2026  


The latest US jobs report shows that the economy added only 17,000 jobs in February 2026. This number is much lower than experts expected. The **job market** is usually a sign of how well the economy is doing. When many jobs are created, it means companies are growing and hiring workers. But a slow increase like this suggests that businesses may be cautious about hiring more people. Also, new college graduates are finding it harder to get jobs, which could affect their future careers. The government and companies will watch these trends closely to see if the job market improves.

## 3. Silicon Valley Debates Closing Borders to Chinese AI Technology  


In Silicon Valley, tech leaders are divided about whether the US should **close the borders** to Chinese artificial intelligence (AI) technology. Some believe stopping Chinese AI products will protect American companies and national security. Others argue that such restrictions could hurt innovation and business partnerships. Meanwhile, China is making big advances in AI, which worries some US companies. The debate shows how important AI has become in global technology and politics. The government must decide how to balance openness with protection in this fast-changing field.

## 4. Courts Block Trump’s Immigrant Detention Policy, Setting Up Supreme Court Fight  


Judges have stopped a policy from the Trump administration that required many immigrants to be held in detention centers. The courts say this policy was too strict and may violate legal rights. This decision could lead to a fight in the Supreme Court, the highest court in the US. The policy focused on mandatory detention, meaning immigrants had to stay in jail-like places while their cases were reviewed. Opponents say this approach is unfair and harms families. Supporters say it helps control immigration. The Supreme Court’s decision will be very important for future immigration rules.

> ### 영어 표현 학습
> - **mandatory detention policy** — 의무 구금 정책  
>   _The court blocked the **mandatory detention policy** for immigrants._  
> - **violate legal rights** — 법적 권리를 침해하다  
>   _Many believe the policy **violates legal rights**._  
> - **set up a Supreme Court fight** — 대법원 싸움을 준비하다  
>   _This case will **set up a Supreme Court fight** over immigration._
