## 1. Lake Powell Hits Record Low Amid Water Crisis  


Lake Powell, a large reservoir in the western United States, has reached its lowest water level ever recorded. This is because of ongoing drought and increased water use in the region. The lake is important because it provides water and electricity to millions of people. If the water level gets too low, it could cause problems for farms, cities, and wildlife. Experts warn that this situation may get worse if the dry weather continues. The government and local groups are discussing ways to save water and protect this vital resource. This crisis shows how climate change and human actions can affect natural resources.

## 2. Trump Administration Tightens Limits on Legal Immigration  


The Trump administration has introduced new rules that **restrict** many types of legal immigration to the United States. This means fewer people may be allowed to enter the country for work, family, or study. Officials say this is to protect American jobs and security. However, critics argue that these restrictions make it harder for families to reunite and for businesses to find workers. The changes could affect thousands of immigrants waiting for approval. This new policy shows how immigration remains a hot political topic in the U.S. and may lead to legal battles.

## 3. US Job Openings Drop While Hiring Grows  


In June, the number of job openings in the U.S. **dropped**, but more people were hired. This means there are fewer new jobs available, but companies are still hiring workers. Experts say this shows the job market is changing. Some industries have fewer jobs to offer, while others need more staff. The hiring increase is good news for many workers looking for jobs. However, the drop in openings could mean companies are more careful about hiring in the future. This report helps us understand how the economy is doing after the pandemic.

## 4. China’s Tech Progress Shakes Silicon Valley and Washington  


China’s recent advances in technology, especially in artificial intelligence (AI), are **causing chaos** in Silicon Valley and the White House. Chinese companies have created powerful and cheap AI tools that challenge U.S. tech firms. Some American companies worry about losing their lead in technology. At the same time, U.S. government officials are trying to decide how to respond. They want to protect national security but also support innovation. This situation shows how global competition in tech is growing and becoming more complicated.
