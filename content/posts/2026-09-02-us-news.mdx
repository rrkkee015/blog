## 1. US Job Openings Rise Slightly in July 

The number of job openings in the United States went up a little bit in July. After a previous month’s report was changed to show fewer job openings, this increase is a sign that employers still want to hire workers. This is important because it shows the **job market** is still strong even with some economic uncertainty. When there are many jobs available, it means workers have more chances to find a good position. Experts will watch the data to see if this trend continues or if the job market changes in the coming months. A stable job market helps the economy grow and gives people confidence about their futures.

## 2. Debates Grow Over US Immigration Policy and Border Security 

In the United States, immigration policy is a hot topic again. Some groups support **remigration**, a far-right idea that means encouraging immigrants to leave or return to their original countries. This idea is influencing how some politicians think about immigration laws. At the same time, the cost of border security and immigration enforcement is very high. Officials are asking Congress to take action on laws that protect immigrants in sanctuary cities. These debates matter because they affect many families and the future of immigration in the US. The government will likely continue to discuss and change immigration policies soon.

## 3. Silicon Valley Explores AI Despite China Sanctions 

Silicon Valley investors are studying how China’s AI (artificial intelligence) industry works. Due to US sanctions, China is trying to become more **efficient** in its technology development. However, experts say it is unlikely that the US and China will completely separate their tech industries. Many companies depend on each other in different ways. The competition between the two countries is sometimes called a “new Cold War,” but some people think this idea is used to excuse **impunity** for tech elites, meaning they avoid responsibility. This story shows how important AI is and how politics affects technology.

## 4. Senator Ed Markey Wins Massachusetts Primary Easily 

In Massachusetts, Democratic Senator Ed Markey won his primary election easily. He was challenged by Representative Seth Moulton but kept a strong lead. Primaries are important because they decide which candidate will represent the party in the main election. Markey’s win shows he has good support from voters in his state. This result affects the balance of power in the US Senate and the future of political issues in Massachusetts. The general election will be the next step for Senator Markey to keep his seat.

> - **challenge** — 도전하다, 경쟁하다

> - **strong lead** — 큰 차이로 앞서다
