## 1. Oil Prices Rise as Middle East Deal Remains Uncertain  


Oil prices went up recently because a deal to secure the Strait of Hormuz has not been made yet. The Strait of Hormuz is a very important waterway for oil shipping. Countries want to keep it safe for trade. But talks to agree on security have been slow and difficult. This uncertainty makes the oil market nervous, so prices **extend gains**. Higher oil prices can affect the cost of gas and goods in the US and around the world. If the deal is made soon, prices might calm down. But for now, investors watch closely as the situation stays unclear.

## 2. New Immigration Rules and What They Mean for You  


The US government has updated immigration rules, especially about the “public charge” rule. This rule affects immigrants who use certain government services. The new rule could make it harder for some people to get green cards if they are seen as likely to **depend on public aid**. Many immigrants and lawyers are watching these changes carefully because they could change who can stay in the US legally. The government says the rule is to protect resources. But critics say it might make life harder for families who need help. It’s important for immigrants to understand these rules and get good advice.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **public charge** — 공공부조 의존자  
>   _The **public charge** rule affects immigrants who use government aid._  
> - **depend on** — ~에 의존하다  
>   _Some immigrants may **depend on** public services._  
> - **watch carefully** — 주의 깊게 지켜보다  
>   _Advocates **watch carefully** how the rule changes._  
> - **get good advice** — 좋은 조언을 받다  
>   _It’s important to **get good advice** from experts._  
> - **protect resources** — 자원을 보호하다  
>   _The government wants to **protect resources** for citizens._

## 3. Silicon Valley Divided Over China and AI Technology  


Silicon Valley is split over how to deal with China when it comes to artificial intelligence (AI). Some tech leaders want to **close the borders** to Chinese AI models, fearing security risks. Others believe that cooperation is important for innovation and business. AI is a growing technology that can change many industries. The debate shows a growing **mutual suspicion** between the US and China. This tension could affect future tech development and trade. Many experts say the US needs a clear plan to balance security and progress.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **close the borders** — 국경을 닫다, 제한하다

> - **mutual suspicion** — 상호 불신  
>   _There is **mutual suspicion** between the US and China._  
> - **split over** — ~에 대해 의견이 갈리다  
>   _Silicon Valley is **split over** how to handle China._

> - **balance security and progress** — 보안과 발전의 균형을 맞추다

## 4. US Job Market Shows Signs of Weakening  


Recent reports show that the US job market is not doing well. In July, the country lost 23,000 jobs, and some earlier numbers were changed to show fewer jobs than first thought. This is a sign of a **weakening labor market**. Experts say this could affect the economy and political opinions. Many workers feel uncertain about their future. Employers seem to be more careful about hiring now. This slowdown may continue if problems like inflation or global issues stay. People are watching closely to see what happens next.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **job market** — 취업 시장  
>   _The **job market** is showing signs of slowing down._  
> - **weakening labor market** — 약해지는 노동 시장  
>   _July numbers point to a **weakening labor market**._  
> - **sign of** — ~의 징후  
>   _Job losses are a **sign of** economic trouble._  
> - **feel uncertain** — 불확실하게 느끼다  
>   _Many workers **feel uncertain** about their jobs._  
> - **watch closely** — 주의 깊게 지켜보다  
>   _Economists **watch closely** for new data._
