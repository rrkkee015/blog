## 1. Trump Shows Off His New Helipad and Talks About Big Projects  


Former President Trump recently showed off a new **helipad** at one of his properties. A helipad is a special place for helicopters to land and take off. Trump also spoke proudly about his many **construction projects** across the country. He said these projects are important for the economy and for creating jobs. People are watching to see if these efforts will help his political image. This news shows how Trump is still very active and wants to be seen as a builder and leader. It might also affect the upcoming elections.

## 2. Trump’s Immigration Policy Reminds Some of the 1920s  


Some experts say that Trump’s immigration policy is very similar to policies from the 1920s. Back then, the US limited certain groups of people from entering the country. Today, Trump’s approach also focuses on stopping some new immigrants. This policy has caused a lot of debate. Some people think it protects jobs and safety. Others say it is unfair and hurts families. Understanding the history helps us see why immigration is still a big issue in America. The debate will likely continue in the months ahead.

> - **immigration policy** — 이민 정책  
>   _The government changed its **immigration policy** last year._

>   _The new rules **limit** the number of visitors._  
> - **debate** — 논쟁, 토론  
>   _There was a long **debate** about the new law._

## 3. American Manufacturers Are Hiring Again — Could Tariffs Be the Reason?  


After a slow period, American manufacturers are starting to hire more workers. Some experts think that tariffs, which are taxes on imported goods, might be helping this growth. Tariffs make foreign products more expensive, so people buy more from American companies. This can create more jobs in factories and workshops. However, not everyone agrees about tariffs. Some say they also cause higher prices for shoppers. The hiring trend is a good sign for the economy but it is still too early to tell how long it will last.  

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **manufacturer** — 제조업체  
>   _The **manufacturer** makes cars and trucks._  
> - **tariffs** — 관세  
>   _The government added **tariffs** on imported steel._  
> - **growth** — 성장, 증가  
>   _There was strong **growth** in the number of new jobs._  
> - **trend** — 경향, 추세  
>   _The hiring **trend** looks positive this month._

## 4. Silicon Valley Companies Clash Over AI Rules and China  


Many big tech companies in Silicon Valley are arguing about how to handle AI technology and its relationship with China. Some companies want to follow strict rules to keep AI secrets safe from China. Others believe closing borders to Chinese AI is not a good idea. This disagreement shows how important AI has become. It also shows the challenge of balancing business and security. The future of AI and international cooperation is still very uncertain.  

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **Silicon Valley** — 실리콘밸리 (미국 IT 산업 중심지)  
>   _Many tech startups are based in **Silicon Valley**._  
> - **AI (Artificial Intelligence)** — 인공지능  
>   _**AI** is changing many industries._  
> - **strict rules** — 엄격한 규칙  
>   _The company follows **strict rules** for data privacy._  
> - **disagreement** — 의견 충돌, 불일치  
>   _There was a **disagreement** about the new policy._
