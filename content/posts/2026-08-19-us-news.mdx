## 1. Florida Primary Election Surprises Shake Up Politics  


The recent primary elections in Florida brought some **unexpected results**. GOP Representative Cory Mills lost his race amid ongoing **investigations**. Meanwhile, Democratic socialist Angie Nixon achieved a surprise win in the US Senate primary. Another interesting twist is former Republican David Jolly winning the Democratic nomination for governor. These results show how political views in Florida are **shifting**. The elections are important because they decide who will run in the general election this fall. Voters seem ready for change, and the candidates will now prepare for the bigger race. It will be interesting to see how this affects Florida’s future policies.

## 2. The High Cost of Immigration Enforcement in the US  


The US government spends a lot of money on immigration enforcement and border security. This means paying for agents, technology, and border walls. A recent report shows the **cost of immigration enforcement** is very high and growing every year. Some people think these expenses are necessary for national safety. Others argue that the money could be used for better immigration policies or helping immigrants. Understanding these costs is important because it affects government budgets and public opinion. The government may need to decide how to balance security with fairness in the future.

## 3. Mixed News on US Job Market in June  


The US job market showed some **mixed signals** in June. Hiring was worse than expected, but the White House said the labor market is on an **upward trajectory**. This means jobs may improve slowly over time. Also, job openings dropped, but hiring increased slightly. Some reports say summer jobs are a “double whammy” for workers because of low wages and fewer opportunities. These facts show the economy is still changing after the pandemic. It is important for workers and employers to watch these trends closely. The government may take action to help create more jobs in coming months.  

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **mixed signals** — 혼재된 신호, 다양한 신호  
>   _The economy is sending **mixed signals** about its strength._  
> - **upward trajectory** — 상승 경로, 향상 추세  
>   _Experts believe the market is on an **upward trajectory** this year._  
> - **double whammy** — 이중 타격  
>   _The new policy was a **double whammy** for small businesses._

## 4. Silicon Valley Divides on Chinese AI Models  


Silicon Valley is **divided** over Chinese companies creating strong and cheap artificial intelligence (AI) models. Some tech leaders want to welcome these AI tools because they are powerful and low cost. Others worry about security and privacy risks if the US relies on Chinese technology. This debate has become a big topic in the tech world and even in the government. Some suggest closing borders to Chinese AI may cause **mutual suspicion** between the US and China. The outcome could affect global AI development and international relations. Many are watching closely how this situation will develop.  

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **divided** — 분열된, 의견이 나뉜  
>   _The community is **divided** on the new policy proposal._  
> - **mutual suspicion** — 상호 불신  
>   _Trade talks failed because of **mutual suspicion** between the countries._  
> - **artificial intelligence (AI) models** — 인공지능 모델  
>   _Companies invest heavily in developing **artificial intelligence (AI) models**._
