## 1. Secret Service Faces Inquiry Over Possible Misconduct  


The U.S. Secret Service is currently under investigation because some officials may have behaved badly while on duty. The agency put several employees on leave while they check what happened. This news is important because the Secret Service protects important leaders like the president. If the rumors of **potential misconduct** are true, it could damage the public’s trust in the agency. The investigation is ongoing, and officials say they will share results when the inquiry is complete. It shows how even trusted groups can face serious questions about their actions. People are watching closely to see what the final decision will be.

## 2. U.S. Immigration Visa Applications Halt Amid New Restrictions  


The U.S. government recently stopped all new applications for immigrant visas. This move comes as the government tightens rules on immigration. Many people who want to move to the United States now face delays and uncertainty. The change affects families and workers who hope to live and work in the country legally. Immigration groups say this policy could harm the economy and separate families. Officials say they want to better control immigration but have not given a clear timeline for when applications will start again. This decision shows how immigration remains a difficult and important topic in the U.S.

## 3. America’s Tech Hubs Are Moving Beyond Silicon Valley  


For many years, Silicon Valley was the center of technology and innovation in the U.S. But now, other cities are growing fast as new hubs for tech companies. Places like Austin, Miami, and Denver attract startups and big tech firms. This change brings new jobs and opportunities to different parts of the country. Experts say this shift helps spread out the tech industry and reduce high costs in Silicon Valley. It also encourages more competition and creativity. This trend is important because it shows how the tech scene in America is becoming more diverse and widespread.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **tech hub** — 기술 중심지  
>   _Seattle is a major **tech hub** with many software companies._  
> - **growing fast** — 빠르게 성장하다  
>   _The city’s population is **growing fast** because of new jobs._  
> - **spread out** — 분산시키다  
>   _Companies want to **spread out** their offices to avoid high costs._  
> - **reduce costs** — 비용을 줄이다  
>   _Remote work helps **reduce costs** for businesses._
