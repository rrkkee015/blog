## 1. US and Saudi Forces Strike Iranian Proxies in Iraq 

The United States and Saudi Arabia recently joined forces to carry out military strikes against Iranian-backed groups in Iraq. These groups have been causing problems in the region, and the strikes aim to reduce their ability to attack. This action shows how the US and its allies are working together to keep the area safer. These strikes could lead to more tension between the countries involved, but they also try to prevent bigger conflicts. It is important because the region is very sensitive and these groups have links to Iran. The US government says this is part of a plan to protect American interests and allies in the Middle East. We might see more military or diplomatic moves in the coming weeks.

Some state lawmakers in the US are working on new rules to protect immigrants. These laws focus on keeping immigrant information private and improving public safety. They also want to change how immigrant detention is handled, making it more fair. These changes are important because immigration is a big and sometimes difficult topic in the US. Many immigrants worry about their privacy and safety. The new laws could help build trust between immigrant communities and local governments. If passed, these rules might change how police and immigration officers work together.

## 3. Louisiana Leads US Construction Job Growth in June 

In June 2026, Louisiana showed the biggest rise in construction jobs in the entire United States. This growth means more people found work in building homes, offices, and other structures. Construction jobs are important because they help the economy grow and provide many opportunities for workers. This increase may be due to new projects and investment in the state. Other states are also seeing job growth, but Louisiana stands out this month. The rise in construction jobs could lead to better local services and more homes for people. This news shows positive signs for the US labor market overall.

## 4. Silicon Valley Divided on Closing Borders to Chinese AI Companies 

There is a big debate in Silicon Valley about whether the US should close its technology borders to Chinese AI companies. Some people want to stop Chinese companies from working with American AI technology because of security concerns. Others believe that closing borders could hurt innovation and business. This debate shows how important and sensitive AI technology is for the future. The US government is thinking about new rules that might limit AI cooperation with China. This discussion could change how companies work together internationally. The decision will affect both the tech industry and relations between countries.

> ### 영어 표현 학습
> - **divided on** — ~에 대해 의견이 갈리다  
>   _People are **divided on** the new policy._  
> - **close borders** — 국경을 닫다, 제한하다  
>   _The government may **close borders** to certain imports._  
> - **security concerns** — 보안 우려  
>   _There are **security concerns** about data privacy._  
> - **hurt innovation** — 혁신을 해치다  
>   _Too many rules can **hurt innovation** in the industry._  
> - **work together internationally** — 국제적으로 협력하다  
>   _Companies often **work together internationally** to share ideas._
