## 1. Trump’s Press Secretary Karoline Leavitt Is Leaving Her Role  


Karoline Leavitt, who worked as the press secretary in Donald Trump’s White House, has announced she will leave her position soon. The press secretary is the person who talks to the media and explains the president’s ideas and plans. Leavitt joined the team during a very busy and challenging political time. Her departure could change how information is shared from the White House. Many people are watching closely because a new press secretary might change the tone and style of communication. This news is important because it shows how political teams often change after big events. We will see who will replace her and how they will handle the job.

## 2. This Week in Immigration: Latest Updates and Policies  


This week, there are important updates about U.S. immigration policies. Immigration means people moving to a new country to live. The government is working on different rules to control who can come and stay in the country. Some policies focus on refugees, who are people escaping danger in their own countries. Others deal with how immigration officers do their job, like through the 287(g) program that lets local police help with immigration enforcement. Understanding these changes is important for many families and workers. Immigration news affects not only people who want to move but also the whole community. We will watch how these rules develop in the coming weeks.

## 3. U.S. Economy Lost 23,000 Jobs Last Month, Surprising Experts  


In a surprising report, the U.S. economy lost 23,000 jobs last month. Usually, Americans expect the number of jobs to grow or stay steady. A job loss means fewer people have work, which can slow down the economy. Experts call this a possible sign of a “wilting labor market,” which means the job market is getting weaker. This news is important because jobs affect how people live and spend money. Some workers may find it harder to get new jobs soon. The government and businesses will watch these numbers closely to plan for the future.

## 4. China’s Tech Growth Creates Challenges for Silicon Valley and U.S. Leaders  


China is making big progress in technology, and this is causing problems for Silicon Valley and the U.S. government. Silicon Valley is a place in California known for many tech companies. The growth of Chinese technology companies brings competition and worry. The White House and U.S. leaders worry about security and losing advantages in technology. This situation creates “mutual suspicion” between the two countries, meaning they don’t fully trust each other. Some U.S. companies want fewer restrictions on working with China, but others want stronger rules. This story shows how technology is important for global power and business.
