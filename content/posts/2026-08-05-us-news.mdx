## 1. Michigan Senate Race Tightens as Candidates Face Off  


The primary election in Michigan is drawing a lot of attention. Two candidates, El-Sayed and Stevens, are **facing off** in the race for the Senate seat. This means they are competing directly against each other to win the party’s nomination. Primary elections are important because they decide who will represent each party in the main election. Voters are watching closely because the winner could have a big impact on future policies. The race has been **heated** with many debates and opinions shared. Both candidates want to show they are the best choice. The final results will shape the political direction in Michigan and possibly the whole country.

## 2. New Public Charge Rule Changes Immigration Process  


The U.S. government introduced a new public charge rule that affects immigrants. This rule looks at whether immigrants might use public benefits, like health care or food assistance, and can affect their chance to get a green card. The change means officials will check more carefully if immigrants might become a **public charge**. This has worried many immigrants and advocates because it could make it harder to stay in the country legally. Supporters say it will protect public resources, but critics argue it could **discriminate** against low-income families. This rule is part of a larger debate about immigration policy in the U.S. and could change how many people apply for permanent residency.

> - **public charge** — 공공부조 수급자

## 3. June Job Market: Fewer Openings but More Hiring  


In June, the U.S. job market showed some interesting trends. Job openings dropped slightly to 7.4 million, but hiring actually increased. This means that while fewer jobs were available, more people found work. Experts say this shows the labor market is still **resilient** despite challenges like conflicts in other countries. A resilient market means it can recover or stay strong even when problems happen. Many businesses are still looking for workers, but they may be more careful in offering jobs. This information helps us understand the health of the economy and what might happen next in employment.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **job openings** — 일자리 공석  
>   _There are many **job openings** in the tech industry._  
> - **hiring** — 고용, 채용

> - **resilient** — 회복력 있는, 탄력적인

## 4. White House and Silicon Valley Clash Over AI Rules  


The White House and Silicon Valley companies are in a **whipsaw** over new rules for artificial intelligence (AI). “Whipsaw” means they are being pulled in different directions or facing conflicting pressures. The government wants to control AI to keep it safe but also wants to support innovation. On the other hand, tech companies want fewer restrictions so they can develop AI faster. This disagreement is causing confusion and delays in creating clear policies. AI is very important because it affects many parts of life and business. How this conflict is solved will shape the future of AI in the U.S.

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **whipsaw** — (상반된 요구로) 곤란한 상황에 처하다  
>   _The company is **whipsawed** between customer demands and regulations._  
> - **artificial intelligence (AI)** — 인공지능  
>   _**Artificial intelligence** is changing many industries._  
> - **innovation** — 혁신  
>   _The government wants to encourage **innovation**._  
> - **restriction** — 제한, 규제  
>   _There are few **restrictions** on this new technology._  
> - **policy** — 정책  
>   _The new **policy** aims to protect users._
