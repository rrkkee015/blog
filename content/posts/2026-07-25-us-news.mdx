## 1. Trump Orders Smithsonian to Add Warnings About U.S. History 

The Trump administration has told the Smithsonian museums to post **warnings** about parts of U.S. history that it calls "**inaccurate**." The warnings say that some history books and museum exhibits show the country in a wrong or unfair way. This order is part of a bigger debate about how America’s history should be told. Some people think it is important to show all sides of history, even the difficult parts. Others say that changing or adding warnings might confuse visitors. The Smithsonian plans to follow the order, but many historians and experts disagree with it. This could affect how people learn about America’s past in the future.

## 2. What Trump’s New Public Charge Rule Means for Immigrants 

The U.S. government has created a new rule called the "public charge" rule. This rule says that some immigrants might be denied a visa or green card if they are likely to use certain public benefits, like food stamps or Medicaid. The rule is meant to make sure immigrants can support themselves without relying on the government. Many immigrants and advocacy groups worry this will make it harder to come to the U.S. or stay legally. It could also affect families and people who need help. Experts suggest immigrants learn about this rule and prepare before applying for visas or benefits. This change is part of the government’s current immigration policy.

## 3. One in Four U.S. Employees Locked in Jobs for Health Insurance 

A recent study shows that about 25% of American workers stay in their jobs mainly because of health insurance. This means they feel **locked in** and do not want to leave their current job. Health insurance is very important in the U.S. because it can be expensive to buy on your own. Many people worry about losing their insurance if they change jobs. This situation can stop workers from finding better jobs or new opportunities. Experts say this is a big problem for the job market and workers’ freedom. Companies and lawmakers may need to find ways to improve health insurance options.
