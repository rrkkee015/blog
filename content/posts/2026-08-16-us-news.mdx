## 1. Hurricane Lala Hits Hawaii with Strong Winds and Heavy Rain  


Hurricane Lala is bringing **torrential rains** and strong winds to Hawaii. This storm is causing damage to homes and roads. Officials are warning people to stay safe and prepare for power outages. Flooding is a big concern because of the heavy rain. The hurricane is moving slowly, which means the bad weather will last longer. Emergency teams are ready to help anyone affected. This storm shows how important it is to be ready for natural disasters.

## 2. Libertarian Proposes Repealing Income Tax and Immigration Rules  


A Libertarian politician wants to **repeal** the income tax, welfare programs, and immigration policy. This means removing these government rules and laws. He believes people should have more freedom with their money and choices. His ideas are very different from current policies. Some people support this because they want less government control. Others worry it could hurt people who need help. This debate shows how immigration and taxes are still very important topics in the US.

## 3. US Economy Lost 23,000 Jobs in July, Showing Hiring Slowed  


In July, the US economy **lost** 23,000 jobs. This means fewer people are working now than before. Experts say this is a sign that **hiring has gone into reverse**. Companies are not hiring as much as they did earlier. This news is surprising because many expected more jobs. The slow job market could affect the economy and politics. People will watch closely to see if this trend continues in the coming months.  

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **lost jobs** — 일자리를 잃다  
>   _Many workers **lost jobs** during the economic crisis._  
> - **hiring has gone into reverse** — 채용이 줄어들다, 반대 방향으로 가다  
>   _After months of growth, **hiring has gone into reverse** this summer._  
> - **job market** — 일자리 시장  
>   _The **job market** is tough for new graduates._  
> - **economic trend** — 경제 동향  
>   _This is an important **economic trend** to watch._

## 4. China’s Technology Growth Shakes Silicon Valley and US Politics  


China is making big **tech advances** that affect Silicon Valley and the White House. These advances include new AI and computer technology. Many people in the US worry about competition and security. Some want to limit Chinese tech companies from working in America. This has caused a split in Silicon Valley, where some support open borders for tech, and others want restrictions. The situation shows how technology is now a big part of global politics and business.  

> ### 영어 표현 학습  
> - **tech advances** — 기술 발전  
>   _The company is known for its fast **tech advances**._  
> - **Silicon Valley** — 실리콘 밸리 (미국 IT 산업 중심지)

> - **split** — 분열, 의견 차이
